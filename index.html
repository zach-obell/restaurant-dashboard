05 / THE RESEARCHER

ZO.

Built from curiosity.
Grounded in the numbers.

Since I started exploring careers in finance, I’ve been drawn to sales and trading and equity research, and especially to the idea of building real expertise in a narrow group of companies. That idea took shape after a conversation with an equity research analyst who covers restaurants. He told me that after roles he didn’t enjoy, covering this industry is what made the work click for him, because it’s interesting and grounded in the real world. This dashboard is my way of testing that for myself. I’m most interested in how news and real-world events show up in these companies’ stock prices, and how restaurant groups move together or apart as conditions change. Keeping it updated is how I’m learning to follow the market in a focused, disciplined way.
